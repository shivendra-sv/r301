  1. 01

    API-first

    No dashboard, no console to click through. Your code creates the links and retires them, over plain REST.

  2. 02

    Served at the edge

    Every redirect is answered by a Cloudflare Worker at the point of presence nearest the click, reading a KV-cached copy of the link.

  3. 03

    Safe by default

    Deleting a link tombstones it: the redirect 404s at once and the slug stays blocked, so nobody inherits a link you retired. Reserved words are blocked. Unknown fields are rejected, not quietly ignored.
